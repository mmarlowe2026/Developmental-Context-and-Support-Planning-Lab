Jordan Lee Week 14 Case

Jordan is a 13-year-old seventh-grade student. One teacher reports frequent movement, unfinished independent work, interruptions, and conflict during unstructured transitions.

Jordan's science teacher reports sustained focus during hands-on activities. A caregiver describes late bedtimes, difficulty starting homework, intense interest in transit maps, sensitivity to cafeteria noise, and strong memory for preferred topics.

Jordan's grades declined after a school change and increased family stress. Jordan says, “People decide I am a problem before they ask what is happening.”

Developmental history, learning and language assessment, peer experiences, mood and anxiety symptoms, trauma exposure, sleep pattern, medical factors, substance exposure, accommodations, and the consistency and duration of concerns remain unclear.

Professional boundary: This fictional information identifies assessment priorities. It does not establish ADHD, autism spectrum disorder, a conduct-related disorder, intellectual disability, or any other diagnosis. Do not diagnose Jordan from limited case details.
Development What is expected for Jordan's age, developmental level, and learning history?
Settings Where does the concern appear, where does it not, and how do demands differ?
Youth Voice What does Jordan notice, value, need, and want adults to understand?
